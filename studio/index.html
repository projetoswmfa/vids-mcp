Step 2

Frames free

Still images: storyboard frames, or the first and last frame of a clip. Turn on Match previous frame so each frame inherits the one before it — that is what keeps a character identical across a set.

Step 3

Shots

Video clips made with Vids' Omni model, 3 to 10 seconds each. Give a shot a first frame and Vids animates it; chain a shot onto the one before it and Vids extends that clip for a continuous move. Every clip uses your Vids generation quota.

Restyle

Change a clip you already have without reshooting it, with Vids' Editar tab: relight it, change the weather or time of day, remove or restyle something. It uses your Vids generation quota.

Step 6

Captions free · local

Put the spoken words on screen, the way short-form videos do, on wide or vertical videos. Nothing is uploaded: this Mac listens to the video and times every word itself.

Drag to place the words anywhere, or tap the preview where you want them.

Pick a video to see your captions on it.

Fix anything misheard: each word keeps its timing. Press Enter to start a new caption at that point.
